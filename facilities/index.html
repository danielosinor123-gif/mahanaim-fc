Proposed development PROPOSED

As published by the club: a comprehensive masterplan on a sprawling 13-hectare site ~10 minutes from Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport (land status, ownership and approvals to be confirmed), comprising:

  1. Four world-class training pitches
  2. Modern hostels for player accommodation
  3. Restaurants and dining facilities
  4. Shopping mall for convenience and leisure
  5. State-of-the-art media and events centre
  6. Camps for team retreats and bonding
  7. Recreation village
  8. Mini estate for staff and officials
  9. Flagship stadium as centrepiece

Development phases TIMELINE TO CONFIRM

Phase 1: pitches + accommodation (proposed) · Phase 2: media/events + dining (proposed) · Phase 3: stadium + estate (proposed). No completion dates claimed.

Intended benefits

Holistic player development, entertainment and community engagement; team camps; safer residential environment — all contingent on funding and approvals.
