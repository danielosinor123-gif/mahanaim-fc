How development works

Who we serve: young footballers in Abuja/FCT seeking disciplined development and a route to competitive and professional football. Parents and guardians are part of the process from day one.

  1. 1. Enquiry / Scouting — register interest via trials hub or be spotted at matches/scouting events. CONFIRMED ROUTE
  2. 2. Assessment or Trial — invited players train/play under observation. PROCESS DETAILS TO CONFIRM (dates, fees if any, what to bring).
  3. 3. Training & Development — football, character, discipline, teamwork. Frequency/curriculum TO CONFIRM.
  4. 4. Matches & Exposure — friendlies, tournaments, media interviews/highlights. See results.
  5. 5. Progression Opportunities — scouting links and reported pathways (e.g. FK Sarajevo, Sigma Olomouc — reported stories). NO GUARANTEE

What we do and don't guarantee

  • We do provide coaching, assessment feedback and competitive exposure for selected players.
  • We do not guarantee selection, contracts, transfers, visas, education, accommodation, scholarships or medical cover.
  • Education, accommodation, scholarships, medical services: not confirmed as currently provided — ask before assuming.
  • Safety: trials follow our safeguarding expectations; U18s must come with parent/guardian consent.
Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee selection or a contract. We reply within ~3 working days.
