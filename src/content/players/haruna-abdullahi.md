Haruna is a tall, agile, and physically dominant attacking midfielder who thrives both on and off the ball. His combative nature makes it extremely difficult for opponents to outwit or dispossess him. He brings a commanding presence to the midfield, often dictating the tempo with his strength and control. Technically, Haruna stands out with natural attacking instincts and excellent awareness. His ability to read the game from all angles of the field allows him to consistently create opportunities. He combines defensive discipline, stamina, and precise positioning with strong ball distribution and creativity. His shooting technique is solid, and he excels in passing, often orchestrating play with maturity beyond his age. Haruna models his style after Federico Valverde, and his performances consistently reflect that inspiration. Tactically, he is versatile and fits well into both 4-4-2 and 4-3-3 systems, depending on the coach's strategy. He frequently steps beyond his primary role, covering large areas of the pitch and contributing defensively and offensively with equal impact. Haruna Abdullahi is a rising talent known for his energy, commitment, and professionalism on the field. He represented Nigeria as part of the U-17 Golden Eaglets squad at the 2023 AFCON in Algeria, further establishing his potential on the international stage.
