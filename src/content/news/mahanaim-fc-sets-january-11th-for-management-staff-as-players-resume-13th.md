The management of Mahanaim Football Club have concluded plans to reopen its offices after management, staffers, as well as players, embarked on the Yuletide season's break, 16th of December, 2022.

The players and staff who have used the break to celebrate Christmas and new Year with their loved ones, would have to reconfigure their minds towards resumption this week as agreed by the club's management.

Making this announcement known is the Club coordinator, Sunday Amoo, who revealed the outcome of the meeting to the media unit without foot-dragging, said,

" Technical and administratively reports have been reviewed properly. Therefore, staffers and players are to return on the 11th and 13th of January, 2023 respectively. Players would be recalled based on the advice from the Technical department and we would start reaching out to them today, 9th of January" he disclosed

Although, there are many exciting things for the players to look forward to as they are all focusing on resumption; Players will be returning to our new Camp base and Training facilities, which are some of the things the management worked on over the last couple of months. As the "technically " SELECTED players are setting sights on resumption this coming weekend, they should as well get ready for the new "Laws " because things would be different this time around .
