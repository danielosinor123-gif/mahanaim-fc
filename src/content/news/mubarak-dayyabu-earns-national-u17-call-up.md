Mubarak Dayyabu has been invited to the camp of Nigeria U17 boys national team, the Golden Eaglets.

Ahead of Nigeria's preparation for the WAFU B U17 Championship, Mahanaim FC left-back, Mubarak Dayyabu, has been invited to the national U17 camp by coach Uwejamomere Eboboritse.

Mubarak's name was contained in a 62-player list released by the Nigeria Football Federation on Wednesday.

Mubarak has been one of Mahanaim FC's outstanding and most consistent players this season. He is expected to report to the national team camp immediately.
