Big Announcement: Lawali Joins MFK Karvina

Mahanaim FC can confirm that Yahaya Danjuma Lawali has joined MFK Karvina

18-year old Lawali is one of the many amazing talents from Mahanaim FC, signed a 4-year deal with Czech top team MFK Karvina.

His impressive talent and leadership saw him included as the vice-captain in the Nigeria U17 national team that won the WAFU-B U17 title in 2022; where he was named in the team of the tournament.

Lawali went on to captain Nigeria's Golden Eaglets at the CAF U17 AFCON, where he was also named in the team of the tournament.

We are therefore delighted to share that he has signed a professional contract with MFK Karvina. Wishing our boy, all the best.
