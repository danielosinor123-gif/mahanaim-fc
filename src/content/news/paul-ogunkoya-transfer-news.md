Official: Paul Ogunkoya completes his move to Stabeak of Norway

Mahanaim FC announced today that it has agreed a deal with Norwegian Club- Stabeak, for the transfer of striker Paul Ogunkoya.

The 18-year-old impressed Stabeak bosses after a Trial, which made them exchange documents with Mahanaim FC for his signature.

Paul Ogunkoya is a disciplined, technical powerful and pacy player. He played a key role in our semifinal finish during the 2022 FCT FA Cup run.

The club wishes Paul Ogunkoya well as he begins his professional career with Stabeak in Norway.
