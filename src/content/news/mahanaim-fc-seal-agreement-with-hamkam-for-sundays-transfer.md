Peter Edache Sunday has completed his move from Mahanaim FC to Norwegian club HamKam, following an agreement between both clubs.

The multi-talented 18 year old Sunday put pen to paper at his new home on Thursday morning, after weeks of observation and evaluation at the Norwegian club in which he impressed.

Sunday is a rough diamond who grew up in Kaduna State- where he was known for his incredible footballing abilities, strong mentality and throwing before Mahanaim FC found him.

The youngster couldn't hide his excitement on signing a professional deal, as he glorified God while speaking with HamKam media.

"I am so happy. I have been waiting for this for a long time and I want to thank God for this moment. I want to make my family, friends and the club proud. HamKam is like a family to me and has stood by me from day one," Sunday said.
