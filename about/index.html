About Mahanaim Football Club

Mahanaim Football Club is a professional association football team based in Abuja, Nigeria's capital city. Officially launched in 2018 (as stated by the club), it was born out of a passion to revitalise African football by returning to fundamentals — quality, longevity and ambition.

Current vs future: everything on this page describes current activities unless marked PROPOSED. The 13-hectare training/stadium complex is a development vision, not completed infrastructure.

Mission, values, philosophy

Our values

Ethics, diversity, inclusiveness, consistency and dignity — developing athletes who live a life of value and purpose.

Our mission

Rebuild the sport's foundations: committed to personal excellence and team success; stronger together, not alone.

Our vision

Goal-oriented athletes who refuse anything less than excellence and hold their own among the greatest.

What the club actually provides

  • Structured training and character/discipline programme
  • Competitive matches and friendlies (see results)
  • Scouting events and tournament exposure
  • Football media: interviews, highlights, transfer stories
  • Partnership routes for clubs, sponsors and community

Not confirmed as provided: education, accommodation, scholarships, medical services. Do not assume these exist — see pathway page and ask via contact.
